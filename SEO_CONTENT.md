## What Is the Studio Pricing Benchmark Tool?

The Studio Pricing Benchmark Tool is a free web application from Poli International that lets tattoo and piercing professionals compare their service prices against regional market benchmarks. The tool covers five regions: UK national average, London/SE England, US national average, Western Europe, and Australia.

Users enter their prices for 10 common services (5 tattoo services and 5 piercing services). The tool then compares each entered price against a benchmark range (minimum to maximum) for the selected region. Results show whether each price is below market, at market rate, or at premium pricing level.

The benchmark data comes from publicly listed studio prices, industry surveys, and artist forums. These represent approximate mid-market ranges. Premium specialists and fine-line artists routinely price above the high end.

## Who Should Use This Tool

**Tattoo studio owners**, Review your entire price list against current market rates during annual pricing reviews.

**Independent tattoo artists**, Check if your hourly rate or session prices align with your region's benchmarks.

**Piercing professionals**, Compare your piercing prices (earlobe, nostril, helix, navel, septum) against regional averages.

**New studio owners**, Establish competitive pricing when opening a new studio by understanding market ranges.

**Artists relocating**, If moving between regions (e.g., UK to Australia), use the tool to understand pricing differences.

## How to Use the Studio Pricing Benchmark Tool

### Step 1, Select Your Reference Region

Use the dropdown menu labelled "Reference region" to choose your market. Options include:
- UK (national average)
- London / SE England
- US (national average)
- EU (Western Europe)
- Australia

The grid of services updates automatically when you change regions, showing the appropriate currency symbol (£, $, €, or A$).

### Step 2, Enter Your Prices

For each service in the grid, enter your current price in the input field. The currency symbol appears before each input. You can enter prices for as many or as few services as you like. Services you skip will show "Not entered" in the results.

Services available:
- Small tattoo (1–2 hrs), Simple design, single colour
- Half-day session (4–5 hrs), Medium-large piece
- Full-day session (7–8 hrs), Large / complex work
- Hourly rate, Per hour billing
- Minimum charge, Walk-in / tiny pieces
- Earlobe piercing (pair), Includes starter jewellery
- Nostril piercing, Includes starter jewellery
- Helix / cartilage, Includes starter jewellery
- Navel piercing, Includes starter jewellery
- Septum piercing, Includes starter jewellery

### Step 3, Click "Compare My Prices"

Press the blue "Compare My Prices" button. The tool validates that at least one price has been entered. If none are entered, an alert prompts you to enter at least one price.

### Step 4, Review Your Pricing Position

The results section displays each service with:
- Your entered price
- The benchmark range for your selected region
- A status badge showing one of three categories:
  - **Below market**, Your price is under the benchmark minimum
  - **Market rate**, Your price falls within the benchmark range
  - **Premium pricing**, Your price exceeds the benchmark maximum

The results section scrolls into view automatically. A note at the bottom explains that reference ranges are approximate and premium specialists may price above the high end.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### 1. What regions does the Studio Pricing Benchmark Tool cover?

The tool covers five regions: UK (national average), London/SE England, US (national average), EU (Western Europe), and Australia. Each region has its own set of benchmark ranges and currency symbols.

### 2. How many services can I compare at once?

You can compare up to 10 services at once: 5 tattoo services (small tattoo, half-day session, full-day session, hourly rate, minimum charge) and 5 piercing services (earlobe pair, nostril, helix/cartilage, navel, septum).

### 3. Do I have to enter prices for all services?

No. You can enter prices for as many or as few services as you like. Services without an entered price will show "Not entered" in the results. You must enter at least one price to run the comparison.

### 4. Where does the benchmark data come from?

Reference ranges are compiled from publicly listed studio prices, industry surveys, and artist forums. They represent approximate mid-market ranges. Premium specialists and fine-line artists routinely price above the high end.

### 5. What do the result badges mean?

- **Below market**, Your price is lower than the benchmark minimum for that service in your selected region.
- **Market rate**, Your price falls within the benchmark range.
- **Premium pricing**, Your price exceeds the benchmark maximum.

### 6. Can I change regions after entering prices?

Yes. When you change the region using the dropdown, the service grid updates with the new region's benchmarks and currency symbol. Any prices you entered remain in the input fields. You need to click "Compare My Prices" again to see results for the new region.

### 7. Is this tool free to use?

Yes, the Studio Pricing Benchmark Tool is completely free. No account or login is required.

### 8. Does the tool save my entered prices?

No. The tool does not save any data. Prices are processed in your browser only and disappear when you refresh or close the page.

### 9. Can I use this tool on mobile devices?

Yes. The tool is built with responsive design and works on desktop, tablet, and mobile browsers.

### 10. How often is the benchmark data updated?

The tool uses static benchmark data embedded in the application. For the most current market rates, Poli International recommends using this tool as a reference alongside your own market research.
