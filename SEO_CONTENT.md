## What is Studio Pricing Benchmark Tool?

The Studio Pricing Benchmark Tool is a free, browser-based reference tool from Poli International that lets a tattoo or piercing studio owner place their own prices against typical price ranges in nine global markets. It shows ranges, never a "correct" price, and it gives no advice on what you should charge.

The tool covers nine reference regions: UK (national average), London / South East England, US (national average), Western Europe, Australia, Central & Eastern Europe, Canada, South America, and South East Asia. Prices switch to that region's currency, so you see GBP, USD, EUR, AUD, CAD, BRL, or THB depending on the region you pick.

For each region, the tool lists ten services: five tattoo services (small tattoo, half-day session, full-day session, hourly rate, and minimum charge) and five piercing services (earlobe pair, nostril, helix / cartilage, navel, and septum). Each service carries a low-to-high range, a confidence label, and a short basis note describing what kind of price it is.

Every row carries a confidence label. **INDICATIVE** means an editorial estimate compiled by Poli International from publicly listed prices, not survey data, and not independently verified. **GAP** means no reliable figure exists, so the row is left blank rather than guessed. Figures were last reviewed in September 2026.

Each region also shows a coverage note explaining what the region's figures represent, and a **Jewellery Inclusions** note explaining whether piercing prices typically include the starter jewellery. For example, UK and London studios generally include basic implant-grade titanium starter jewellery, while US and Canadian studios typically charge a piercing service fee with jewellery purchased separately.

Beyond the benchmark table, the tool includes a **Compare My Prices** panel where you type your own price for any service and see whether it sits Below Market, at Market Rate, or Premium. It also includes a **Your Rates** panel for your hourly rate, minimum charge, shop minimum, and deposit percentage, a **What-If Rate Calculator** with a slider from -20% to +30%, a plain-text settings handoff block, a printable Studio Rate Card, a visual comparison chart, and a CSV export.

Everything runs in your browser. Nothing you enter is sent anywhere. Your prices and rates are saved in your own browser, so they are still there next time. Clearing your browser's site data removes them.

## How to Use the Studio Pricing Benchmark Tool

### Step 1: Choose a Reference Region

Use the **Reference region** selector to pick one of nine markets: UK (national average), London / SE England, US (national average), Western Europe, Australia, Central & Eastern Europe, Canada, South America, or South East Asia. The table's prices switch to that region's currency. A **Coverage Scope** note appears below the selector explaining what the region's figures represent, and a **Jewellery Inclusions** note explains whether piercing prices in that region typically include starter jewellery.

### Step 2: Read the Benchmark Table

The **Benchmark Table** tab lists all ten services for the selected region. Each row shows the service name, a short description, the low-to-high range in the region's currency, a confidence label (INDICATIVE or GAP), and a basis note describing what kind of price it is. A **Print Benchmark** button lets you print the reference table.

### Step 3: Enter Your Own Service Prices and Compare

Switch to the **Compare My Prices** tab. Type your own price for any service in the **Your Price** field. Click **Compare My Prices**. Each service is placed as **Below Market** (under the range), **Market Rate** (within it), or **Premium** (above it). A service with no data shows **Data Gap**. A summary counts how many of your services fall in each position. **Clear All Rates** empties every price you entered.

### Step 4: Set Your Studio Rates

In the **Your Rates** panel, enter the four numbers that define your studio:

- **Hourly rate**
- **Minimum charge**
- **Shop minimum**
- **Deposit percentage**

Choose a **studio tier**: Apprentice, Resident Artist, Established Artist, or Specialist / Guest Artist. The tier only adds a description of that kind of artist; it does not change any figure. The panel shows whether your hourly rate and your shop minimum sit below, within, or above the region's range.

### Step 5: Test a Rate Change with the What-If Calculator

Move the slider from **-20%** to **+30%** to adjust your hourly rate, and set the length of a typical booking in hours (2 by default). The calculator shows the adjusted hourly rate, the price of that booking, and the deposit due on it at your deposit percentage. It is arithmetic on your own numbers only. It does not predict what the market will do.

### Step 6: Copy Your Numbers into Your Studio Settings

The **Your Numbers** block is a plain-text summary listing your hourly rate, minimum charge, shop minimum, deposit percentage, and tier, ready to copy into your booking system or studio settings with the **Copy** button. It is labelled as your decision, not a recommendation from the tool. A field you have left blank shows a dash, never a default figure.

### Step 7: Print a Studio Rate Card

Print a one-page **Studio Rate Card** (A4 or US Letter) from your own numbers and service prices, dated today. The card carries only your figures, not the regional ranges.

### Step 8: Review the Visual Comparison Chart

After you compare, a bar chart plots your price for each service beside the range's low and high values. Filter it to **All Procedures**, **Tattoo Only**, or **Piercing Only**.

### Step 9: Export the Comparison as CSV

**Export CSV** downloads the full comparison for a spreadsheet: region, service, currency, range, confidence, basis note, jewellery policy, your price, and its position.

- **Small tattoo (1–2 hrs):** simple design, single colour, palm-size or smaller
- **Half-day session (4–5 hrs):** medium-large piece or detailed single placement
- **Full-day session (7–8 hrs):** large / complex work, sleeve progression, back piece

- **Nostril piercing:** standard nostril placement with stud or ring
- **Helix / cartilage:** upper ear cartilage placement

- **Western Europe, Central & Eastern Europe, South America:** includes standard initial starter jewellery
- **South East Asia:** includes basic initial starter jewellery
- **US, Canada:** piercing fee only, jewellery charged separately

## Use-Case Examples

### Example 1: A UK Studio Reviewing Its Hourly Rate

A studio outside London charges £95 per hour. They select the **UK (national average)** region, where the hourly rate range is £80 to £180. They enter £95 in the **Your Price** field for the hourly rate row and click **Compare My Prices**. The tool places them as **Market Rate**. In the **Your Rates** panel they enter their hourly rate of £95, a minimum charge of £60, a shop minimum of £50, and a deposit percentage of 20%. The panel confirms their hourly rate sits within the UK range. They use the **What-If Rate Calculator** to test a +10% adjustment, which shows an adjusted hourly rate of £104.50 and a two-hour booking price of £209.00 with a £41.80 deposit.

### Example 2: A London Piercer Checking Nostril Pricing

A London piercer charges £55 for a nostril piercing. They select **London / SE England**, where the nostril range is £50 to £90. They enter £55 and the tool places them as **Market Rate**. The **Jewellery Inclusions** note confirms that London studios generally include basic implant-grade titanium starter jewellery, which matches their own policy. They then check their septum price of £45 against the London range of £55 to £110 and see they are placed as **Below Market**, prompting a review.

### Example 3: A US Studio Testing a 15% Rate Increase

A US studio charges $150 per hour. They select **US (national average)**, where the hourly range is $100 to $250, placing them as **Market Rate**. In the **Your Rates** panel they enter $150 hourly, a $75 minimum charge, a $50 shop minimum, and a 25% deposit. They move the **What-If Rate Calculator** slider to +15%, which shows an adjusted hourly rate of $172.50. With a typical booking of 3 hours, the calculator shows a booking price of $517.50 and a deposit due of $129.38. They copy the resulting numbers into their booking system using the **Copy** button, then **Export CSV** to keep a record of the comparison.
